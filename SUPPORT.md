# Support

This is the organization-wide default for repositories without a local support
policy. A repository may provide more specific support channels, service
levels, or version policies.

## Before asking

1. Identify the repository closest to the problem.
2. Read its README, documentation, examples, and troubleshooting guidance.
3. Search open and closed issues for the same question or behavior.
4. Collect a minimal, sanitized example and relevant environment details.

## Ask for help

Use the affected repository's **Question** issue template when available.
Otherwise follow its documented public support channel or the issue form
closest to support. Keep one focused question per issue.

Include:

- the goal and expected outcome;
- what you tried and what happened;
- relevant versions, configuration, and environment;
- the smallest reproducible example available; and
- sanitized logs or screenshots when useful.

Community support is best effort. Opening an issue does not guarantee a response
time, fix, roadmap commitment, or support for an unmaintained version.

## Use the correct private path

- Suspected vulnerabilities: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never post
  details publicly.
- Conduct concerns: follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Account, billing, or GitHub platform problems: contact the relevant service
  provider rather than an organization project.
